Loop earplugs come with swappable inserts that add extra damping. The inserts are useful when you want them, but easy to misplace when you take them out. I wanted to give them a dedicated place inside the case, without making the case harder to use.

The holder needed to do three things: let an insert go in and out easily, keep it in place while it was not being used, and leave enough clearance for the earplugs and the closed lid.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/images_loop_insert/render_loop.PNG" caption="loop insert render." size="medium" %}


## Iterations

### different sizes

Printed single posts in a range of diameters and tried each by hand, judging how firmly the insert sat and how easily it came off.

### cord cutout

The first holder also had a small cutout for a cord. The idea was to give me something to pull when removing an insert. In practice, the cord pullout method didn't work because the inserts material is to flexible.

### Orientation change

I dropped the cutout and put the loop inserts with the small face down. The wider face gives the fingers a grip. Easy solution.
