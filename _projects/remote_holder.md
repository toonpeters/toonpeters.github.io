I like to make holders for everything and have it organized. I was constantly losing the light remote of all the lights in my room. So I printed a simple holder where the light remote clicks in.

## Requirements  
The light remote should:
- click in perfectly
- stay put when clicked in
- easily removed


## Iterations 
The part that took the most time was iterations for different widths of the holders and the design of the top end so the remote perfectly "clicks" in. 

<div class="figure-grid">
{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/images_remote_holder/IMG_8469.jpg" caption="Checking the fit with the actual light remote" size="medium" %}
{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/images_remote_holder/IMG_8868.jpg" caption="Iterations to get the right dimensions for the claw" size="medium" %}
</div>


## The result
2 sided tape was used to attach it to the wall. I created a version with wings on the side to make it a bit more cool looking. This has no extra functionality.
