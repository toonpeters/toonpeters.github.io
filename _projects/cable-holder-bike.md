I was working on my girlfriends new bike, a old aluminium mountainbike. She took all the components off and I built the bike up again with new or restored parts. When cleaning the frame I saw that the cable guide just before the derailleur was broken. This is one of the most important cable guides as the wire must have no resistance for proper shifting. Perfect opportunity to fix this with a 3D print.

## Requirements  
- good diameter of tube piece so it grips firmly to the bike
- use a integrated hinge system for ease of use and install
- shift cable holder on the side must go up at an angle so the shift cable makes minimum bends to the derailleur. This is very important this is for smooth shifting. 
- use threaded inserts to clamp down the pipe holder. 


## Iterations 
the starting angle of the shifter cable. I printed some small guides to check the correct bike tube diameter, starting and ending angle of the cable guide. The cable guide is put at an angle upwards so it needs less bending when going in the derailleur. This angle is clearly shown in the 2D drawing. 

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/images_cable_holder_bike/size_iteration.png" caption="3D model of the cable holder." size="medium" %}



## The result
It was interesting integrating the hinge mechanism and printing it in one go. The spacing between the hinge parts took some trail and error to get it right.
